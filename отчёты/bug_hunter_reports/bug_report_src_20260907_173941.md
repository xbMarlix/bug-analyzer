- **File:** `Funding.sol:130`
- **Severity:** HIGH
**Problem:** The `claim` function uses floor-rounding in the per-share accumulator, which can lead to a few wei above the actual funding deposited over many share changes.
EXPLOIT: An attacker can repeatedly deposit and withdraw small amounts to exploit the rounding error and claim more funding than they are owed.
IMPACT: Potential loss of funds for LPs due to rounding errors in funding distribution.
