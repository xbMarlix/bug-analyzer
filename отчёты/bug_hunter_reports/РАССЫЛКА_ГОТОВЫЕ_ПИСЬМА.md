```
Hello Panoptic team,

I ran a quick security screening of the current panoptic-v2 contracts
(d65310d) and one thing stood out as worth a documented decision:

OraclePack.sol:548
    currentEpoch = (block.timestamp >> 6) & 0xFFFFFF; // 64-long epoch, mod 2**24
    timeDelta = int256(uint256(uint24(currentEpoch - recordedEpoch))) * 64;

Your volatility oracle timestamps observations with a 24-bit epoch
(64s granularity). The uint24 wraps after ~34 years, and the arithmetic
difference is forced through uint24 — so any two observations farther

options protocol where the EMA-weighted median tick is the core price
input, that's the classic narrow-clock class; same family as the
uint32 timestamps that overflow in 2106.

Obvious note: not a today-problem. But since this clock feeds
premiums and liquidation bounds, a uint256/uint64 clock would remove
the whole class, and it's much cheaper to document now than after V2
is live at scale.

Full report: 25 findings with severity mapping and the honest
false-positive filter (most HIGH are benign). $800, 48h.

Best regards,
iskra.mod
```

**Тизер: LambdaHook.sol:437 — весь нативный ETH-баланс контракта (вкл. накопленные комиссии LP от native-token0 пулов) уходит любому depositor.**

```
Hello Janhavi (Lambda),

Quick security screening of the current lambda-protocol contracts
(LambdaHook src), one thing stands out as worth a documented decision:

LambdaHook.sol:437  (deposit)
    // Refund any native surplus (token0 paid from msg.value).
    if (address(this).balance > 0) SafeTransferLib.safeTransferETH(msg.sender, address(this).balance);

deposit is payable and refunds THE ENTIRE contract balance to the
caller, whenever there is any surplus on the hook. But the hook is
designed to also custody accrued LP fees (pendingFees0/1, swept via
collectFees) — and for native-token0 pools those fees settle into the
contract in ETH (settle{value} + receive()). Trade enough in a
WETH/… pool and the deposited fee balance is swept by the *next*
depositor, not by the LPs who earned it.

I.e. the same msg.value covers both the deposit and effectively
transfers a prize taken directly out of the vault's fee escrow. Not
grief here — a direct loss to existing LPs whenever any ETH fees have
accrued and a top-up call settles.

Refund only the caller's own surplus (balanceBefore - settled), not
the contract's whole balance, and pay fees via their dedicated path.

Since mainnet is still ahead of you, this is a good one to fix before
deploy. Full report (12 findings + honest false-positive filter) —
$400, 24h.

Best regards,
iskra.mod
```
